I.

The Ensemble

Andante — with warmth

Formed by cellist Justin Pearson, the Locrian Ensemble of London draws together some of the capital's most prominent string soloists — players whose individual flair shapes every performance of the great chamber and orchestral repertoire.

The ensemble established its long-running concert series at St Martin-in-the-Fields on Trafalgar Square, and has taken theatre concerts and festival performances across Britain, alongside an extensive catalogue of recordings and broadcasts.

Justin Pearson is principal cellist of the National Symphony Orchestra UK and an Associate of the Royal Academy of Music — and the Locrian sound carries that pedigree: precise, expressive, and unmistakably live.

Justin Pearson's powerful, roomy tone… stunning advocacy, combining lyricism of heartfelt intensity with an acute sensitivity to the music's harmonic progress.
